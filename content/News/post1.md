# MUNIKA at MDS 2026: Karlsruhe Delegation Takes Munich

From April 9th to 12th, 2026, the city of Munich became a hub of diplomacy, debate, and international collaboration as it hosted the **Munich Diplomacy Summit (MDS)**. Among the many delegations in attendance was a dynamic group of ten students from Karlsruhe, representing **MUNIKA (Model United Nations Initiative Karlsruhe)**.

Over the course of four days, MUNIKA delegates immersed themselves in a wide array of committees, ranging from the fast-paced negotiations of the **United Nations Security Council (UNSC)** and **NATO**, to the historically rich setting of the **Roman Senate**, and the policy-driven discussions within **BRICS+**, **SPECPOL**, **UNESCO**, and the **US Senate Committee on Foreign Relations**. Each committee offered its own unique challenges, demanding adaptability, critical thinking, and strong diplomatic skills.

The delegation distinguished itself not only through active participation and thoughtful debate, but also through leadership. Notably, MUNIKA member **Mahima Rajpal** served as a Chair in the **BRICS+ committee**, guiding discussions and ensuring a high standard of discourse throughout the sessions—an achievement that reflects both personal dedication and the strength of the organization as a whole.

Beyond formal sessions, the conference provided valuable opportunities for cultural exchange and connection with fellow delegates from across the globe. For many, MDS 2026 was not just about policy and position papers, but also about building lasting networks and gaining new perspectives.

With ten committed delegates and representation across all major committees, MUNIKA’s presence at MDS 2026 underscored its growing role within the European MUN circuit. The experience in Munich will no doubt continue to shape its members as they carry forward the spirit of diplomacy back to Karlsruhe.

# MUNIKA Delegation Report – Munich Diplomacy Summit (MDS) 2026

## Overview

The **MUNIKA (Model United Nations Initiative Karlsruhe)** delegation participated in the **Munich Diplomacy Summit (MDS) 2026**, held in Munich from **April 9th to April 12th, 2026**. The delegation was sent from Karlsruhe and consisted of **10 members**, representing the organization in a diverse range of committees.

## Conference Details

- **Conference Name:** Munich Diplomacy Summit (MDS)
- **Location:** Munich, Germany
- **Dates:** April 9–12, 2026

## Committees

MUNIKA delegates actively participated in the some of following committees:

- US Senate Committee on Foreign Relations
- United Nations Security Council (UNSC)
- Roman Senate
- BRICS+
- NATO
- Special Political and Decolonization Committee (SPECPOL)
- United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO)

## Delegation Participation

The delegation demonstrated strong engagement across all committees, contributing to debates, drafting resolutions, and collaborating with international participants.

A special highlight of the conference was the role of **Mahima Rajpal**, a member of MUNIKA, who served as a supporting **Chair in the NATO committee** for the first time at MDS.

Our very own Member **Akshat** got an honorable mention.

## Conclusion

The participation of MUNIKA at MDS 2026 marked another successful representation of Karlsruhe in international Model United Nations conferences. The delegation not only strengthened its diplomatic and negotiation skills but also reinforced its presence within the European MUN community. Thankyou
